# 0002 · Authorization and revocation

PostgreSQL owns direct grants by principal and document. Filter by eligibility before candidate cutoff, recheck context reuse and read the authority again before private delivery. A missing or unavailable authority fails closed. The public route must preserve confidentiality of hidden documents.

The witness tests grant and revocation before context and before final return. Target transport acceptance requires timing from committed revocation to exclusion across retrieval, caches and delivery. Exercise stale ACL copies, separate identities and the final-read-to-emission race.
