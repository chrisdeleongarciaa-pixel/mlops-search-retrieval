# 0003 · Citations and response delivery

Accumulate private output and release it after citation validation and final authorization. The public contract uses provisional tokens and an explicit terminal event. Disconnect closes the backend iterator and releases admission capacity. Distinguish whether a citation resolves to the effective prompt from whether its content supports a claim.

Tests cover invented or truncated references, authorization failure and disconnect. The real CPU witness additionally resolves the citation to original bytes. Semantic support and transport behavior under load remain separate acceptance work. [Delivery evidence](../../docs/verification/integration-gate.md)
